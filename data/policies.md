# Saffron & Thread: support knowledge base

Saffron & Thread is a fictional handloom home-textiles shop in Jaipur, created for this demo.

## Returns: full-price items
Full-price items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, unwashed and returned with their original tags and packaging. Bedsheets must be returned together with their matching pillow covers. To start a return, email returns@saffronandthread.example with the order number and two photos of the item. We reply within 1 business day with a return label or a pickup date.

## Final Sale items
Items marked Final Sale can only be exchanged for a different size or colour within 7 days of delivery. Final Sale items are not eligible for a refund.

## Items that cannot be returned
Made-to-order and personalised items, including monogrammed linen and custom-size curtains, cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective. Gift cards are non-refundable.

## Return pickup fee
For returns within India we arrange a doorstep pickup. A pickup fee of ₹149 is deducted from the refund. The pickup fee is waived if the item arrived damaged or defective. International customers ship returns back at their own cost.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, email support@saffronandthread.example within 7 days of delivery with the order number and photos. We arrange a free pickup and offer a replacement or a full refund, including the shipping charge.

## Refund timelines
Once a return reaches our Jaipur warehouse, we inspect it and process the refund within 5–7 business days to the original payment method. Cash on Delivery (COD) orders are refunded by UPI or bank transfer. Customers who choose store credit instead of a refund get a 10% bonus, and store credit never expires.

## Order changes and cancellations
An order can be changed or cancelled within 12 hours of placing it, as long as it has not been dispatched. After dispatch it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned under the returns policy.

## Shipping within India
Shipping is free on orders of ₹1,999 or more; below that a flat charge of ₹99 applies. Orders are dispatched within 2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3–6 business days to metro cities and 5–9 business days to other towns. Express delivery costs ₹249 and takes 1–3 business days in 12 metro cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Chandigarh, Lucknow and Kochi.

## Cash on Delivery
Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to ₹10,000. A COD handling fee of ₹49 applies.

## International shipping
We ship to 28 countries through DHL Express, including the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia, the UAE, Singapore and most of the European Union. International shipping starts at ₹1,850 for parcels up to 1 kg and delivery takes 7–12 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer when the parcel arrives.

## Tracking
Customers receive a tracking link by SMS and email within 24 hours of dispatch. We cannot deliver to PO boxes.

## Late or lost parcels
If a parcel has not arrived 5 business days after the latest estimated delivery date, the customer should write to us and we open an investigation with the courier. Claims for lost parcels must be raised within 15 days of the estimated delivery date. If the courier confirms the parcel is lost, we send a free replacement or a full refund.

## Care: handloom cotton
Wash new handloom cotton separately in cold water for the first two washes, because natural dyes may release a little colour. Use a mild liquid detergent and never bleach. Dry in the shade. Handloom cotton may shrink by up to 3% after the first wash; our sizes allow for this.

## Care: Dabu and block-printed fabrics
Hand wash Dabu and block-printed fabrics for the first three washes. After that a gentle machine cycle in cold water is fine. Dry in the shade.

## Care: silk and jute
Silk cushion covers are dry clean only. Do not machine wash or soak jute rugs; spot clean with a damp cloth and mild soap, and rotate the rug every 3 months.

## Warranty
Curtains and cushion covers carry a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects such as broken zips, loose seams and faulty eyelets. It does not cover fading, normal wear or damage from washing against the care instructions.
